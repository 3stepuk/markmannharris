Mark Mann-Harris

Mark Mann-Harris trained as a nurse, then as a midwife — one of the first men to qualify in Britain. He spent thirty years in birth work: supporting families, training birth workers, and teaching men how to be present in the birth room.

He is the founder of Birthing 4 Blokes and the creator of the Birthing Awareness 3-Step process, and the author of Men, Love & Birth (2015; a second edition in 2026).

Alongside that, he is an NLP trainer and clinical hypnotherapist, and works on what he calls neuro-semantic phenomenology — the way language, meaning and lived experience shape one another.

He writes about birth and language, about neuro-semantic phenomenology and NLP, and about the faith he is exploring.

He is the father of eight and a grandfather of thirteen, and lives in Leicestershire.
