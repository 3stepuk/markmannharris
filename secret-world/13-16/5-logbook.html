Module 5

The Logbook

Six real conversations. Watch which question does the work.

Why read other people's conversations

Things happen fast in a real conversation. A map can change in a sentence, or a trap can refuse to move for ten minutes.

These six are written out line by line, so you can see which tool was used and what it did. Read them in order. The first one has nothing wrong in it at all, and it is the one to read first.

Log 0

The shed

Nothing is wrong in this one. Tools: the Magnifying Glass, the Flashlight, the Map Pin, the Rewind, the Compass.

Ellis is sitting on a wall with a friend, and neither of them has a problem. The friend asks what he'd like to explore, and Ellis says, “My grandad's shed.”

Navigator

“Your grandad's shed. And what kind of shed is that shed?”the Magnifying Glass

Ellis

“It's small and cold, but there's a heater on. It smells of sawdust and petrol.”

Navigator

“Small and cold with a heater on, and it smells of sawdust and petrol. And is there anything else about that shed?”the Flashlight

Ellis

“There's a radio on the shelf that's always on. It's really quiet, so you can only just hear it.”

Navigator

“A radio you can only just hear. And where do you feel that shed?”the Map Pin, to the body

Ellis

“In my shoulders. They go down.”A Body Clue.

Navigator

“And your shoulders go down. And what happens just before you get to the shed?”the Rewind

Ellis

“The gravel. You can hear the gravel under the door.”

Navigator

“And what would help you find your way back to that shed when you're not there?”the Compass, as a way back

Ellis

“Sawdust. Or the radio, really quiet.”

What happened. Nothing was fixed, because nothing was broken. Ellis now has a place with a shape, a body feeling and two ways in. That is worth having before you need it.

Log 1

The weight

Tools: the Map Pin, the Magnifying Glass, the Compass, the Flashlight.

Mia is staring at her maths homework and starts crying. “It's just too much. It feels like a giant weight.”

Navigator

“And it's too much, and it feels like a giant weight. And where is that weight?”the Map Pin

Mia

“It's pushing right down on my shoulders.”

Navigator

“Pushing down on your shoulders. And what kind of giant weight is that?”the Magnifying Glass

Mia

“It's a huge grey concrete block. It's so heavy I can't lift my pencil.”

Navigator

“And it's a huge grey concrete block, and you can't lift your pencil. And when that block is on your shoulders… what would you like to have happen?”the Compass

Mia

“I just want it to break into pieces so I can move.”

Navigator

“And you want it to break into pieces. And is there anything else about breaking into pieces?”the Flashlight

Mia

“If I just do the first problem, the block cracks a bit. If I do another, it crumbles into sand.”A breath, and she sits up.

What happened. The Navigator never said “maths isn't that hard” or “just do the first one”. They mapped the block. Once Mia could see it crumble, she picked up the pencil.

Log 2

The hallway

Tools: the Rewind, the invisible strings, the Compass.

Leo and Jayden had a misunderstanding. Now every time they pass in the corridor, Jayden freezes. “It's so awkward.”

Navigator

“And it's awkward, and you freeze. And what happens just before you freeze?”the Rewind

Jayden

“I see Leo walking towards me, and this spiky invisible wall comes up between us.”

Navigator

“And a spiky invisible wall comes up. And is there a connection between Leo and that wall?”the invisible strings

Jayden

“No… the wall comes from me. It's like a porcupine. It comes up because I'm scared he's still angry.”

Navigator

“And it comes up because you're scared he's angry. And when that spiky wall is there, like a porcupine… what would that wall like to have happen?”the Compass, on the landmark

Jayden

“It just wants to protect me. But it doesn't need to be spiky. It could be a soft bubble.”

Navigator

“And it could be a soft bubble. And when it's a soft bubble… what happens to the freezing?”letting it settle

Jayden

“I don't freeze. I can just wave at him.”

What happened. The Rewind found the trigger. The connection showed that the wall was trying to help. It needed a different shape, not removing.

Log 3

The blank screen

Tools: the room.

Sam is trying to write a story and is completely stuck. “My brain is empty. It's just a blank screen.”

Navigator

“And your brain is empty, and it's a blank screen. And where is that blank screen?”

Sam

“It's right in front of my nose. I can't see anything else.”The angle is too close.

Navigator

“Right in front of your nose. And if you could move to any spot in this room to look at that blank screen, where would you go?”the room

Sam

“I'd sit on the windowsill, far from my desk.”Gets up and sits there.

Navigator

“And from there… what do you know now about that blank screen?”

Sam

“Oh. It's not a screen. It's a projector screen, and the projector is behind my chair. The screen is blank because I haven't turned the projector on.”

Navigator

“And you haven't turned the projector on. And what needs to happen to turn it on?”

Sam

“I just need to write down one terrible idea to get it running.”

What happened. The screen was too close to his face. Walking to the window zoomed out, and the whole system came into view.

Log 4

The tryout

Tools: the two hands, and the question in the middle.

Chloe is panicking about the football team. “If I try out, I might not make it and I'll be embarrassed. But if I don't, I'll be furious with myself. I'm torn in half.”

Navigator

“And you're torn in half. Let's map it. Put ‘trying out and being embarrassed’ in your left hand. What does that hand feel like?”

Chloe

“It's heavy. Like a freezing cold rock.”

Navigator

“And a freezing cold rock. Now put ‘not trying out and being furious’ in your right hand. What does that feel like?”

Chloe

“It's hot. Like a buzzing hornet.”

Navigator

“A cold rock here, and a buzzing hornet there. Hold them both out. Now look at the empty space in the middle. And what is between the cold rock and the hornet?”the question in the middle

Chloe

“There's… a path. A grassy path.”

Navigator

“And a grassy path. And what kind of grassy path is that?”

Chloe

“It's just a path where I go out and kick the ball because I love playing. It has nothing to do with the team or the coach.”Her hands drop. “I don't have to carry the rock or the hornet. I can just walk on the grass.”

What happened. Advice — “just do your best” — would have kept her holding both. The space between the traps had a path in it.

Log 5

The solo

Tools: paper, and the door to tomorrow.

Maya is alone in her room. Tomorrow she has to sing a solo at the school concert, and she feels sick with it. There is nobody to ask her the question, so she does it herself.

Maya

1. She asks herself: “When I'm terrified about the solo, what is that like?” She draws a tight, jagged knot of black scribbles in the middle of the page.

Maya

2. She asks the drawing: “What kind of black knot is that?” It's a knot of electric wire. She draws yellow lightning coming off it.

Maya

3. “When this electric knot is here, what would I like to have happen?” On the other side of the page she draws a smooth, glowing bulb.

Maya

4. “What needs to happen for the knot to become the bulb?” She stares at it. The wire doesn't need to disappear. It needs to plug in. She draws a plug, and takes a long breath.

Maya

5. “And as I take this bulb with me… what is the very first thing I will notice when I walk on stage tomorrow?”

Maya

“I'll notice the microphone stand in the spotlight, and I'll feel the electricity run down my arms and into my feet.”

What happened. She untangled the trap on paper, then put the result in tomorrow. When she walked out, it was already there.
