The Secret World

Everyone carries a world nobody else can see. This is how you learn to move around in your own — and how to help someone else move around in theirs without trampling it.

The modules

Start with the place that holds

  1. 00The Safe ZoneA place on your map that already holds — and the discovery that nobody else's world looks quite like yours.
  2. 01The Navigator's GuideThe basics: landmarks, the thread, the three rules, and the six questions.
  3. 02The Expansion PackInvisible strings, the Glitch Trap, using the room, and letting a change settle.
  4. 03The Long ViewTrusting the engine, carrying it into tomorrow, and working on your own map.
  5. 04On Your OwnFive ways to get the map out of your head when there is nobody to ask you the question.
  6. 05The LogbookSix real conversations, line by line, with the question that did the work.
  7. 06The Field WorksheetOne sheet. Fill it in and print it, or use it as a guide on paper.
  8. 07The Translation MatrixEvery word in the programme, set against the clinical term it stands for.
  9. 08The Facilitator's GuideFor the adult in the room: how to teach this without teaching them to be mini-therapists.

For adults

If you're the one holding the map

The material is written for the young person, not for you. The adult notes sit at the foot of each module and on two pages of their own: for parents, and for teachers and practitioners.

If you want the thinking underneath — where the method came from, what it rests on, and an honest account of what is and isn't known — read The philosophy, in plain language.

There is a separate edition of this programme written for 8 to 12. It is a different set of pages, with a different register, and it lives here.
