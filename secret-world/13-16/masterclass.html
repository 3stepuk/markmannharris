Reference

The Masterclass

All twelve keys in one conversation, in three phases.

Before you read it

Most conversations need two or three keys. This one uses all twelve, because the thing underneath it is deep.

Read it second, not first. Before you try a conversation like this, run Module 0 on a place that holds — the same twelve keys, on easy ground, with nothing wrong. Then come back.

Alex is the Navigator. Sam is the friend, and the panic is about a school play audition tomorrow.

Phase 1

Building the map

Sam

“I'm so stressed about the audition tomorrow. I feel this huge pressure.”A landmark appears: pressure.

Alex · Key 2 · the Magnifying Glass

“And you feel a huge pressure. And when you feel a huge pressure… what kind of pressure is that pressure?”

Sam

“It's a tight, squashing pressure.”

Alex · Key 5 · the Shape-Shifter

“A tight, squashing pressure. And that squashing pressure is like what?”

Sam

“It's like a giant, heavy rubber band wrapping around me.”

Alex · Key 3 · the Map Pin

“Like a giant, heavy rubber band. And where is that rubber band?”

Sam

“It's wrapped around my middle.”

Alex · Key 4 · the Radar Scan

“Around your middle. And whereabouts around your middle?”

Sam

“Right over my ribs. It's squeezing them together.”

Alex · Key 1 · the Flashlight

“Squeezing your ribs. And is there anything else about that heavy rubber band?”

Sam

“Yeah, it's neon green. And it's vibrating, like it's humming with electricity.”

Phase 2

Time

Alex · Key 8 · the Rewind

“Neon green and humming with electricity. And what happens just before it starts humming and squeezing?”

Sam

“I think about walking out onto the empty stage and seeing the drama teacher looking at me.”

Alex · Key 9 · the Origin Tracker

“You think about the empty stage and the teacher. And where could that neon green rubber band come from?”

Sam

“Honestly? It comes from my older brother. He told me I was going to mess up and embarrass myself. He gave me the rubber band.”

Alex · Key 6 · the Play Button

“It comes from your older brother. So he gives you the rubber band, and it squeezes your ribs… and then what happens?”

Sam

“I stop taking deep breaths. I just freeze.”

Alex · Key 7 · the Fast-Forward

“You freeze. And what happens next?”

Sam

“I guess I hide in my room and don't practise my lines. It keeps me stuck.”

Phase 3

Changing it

Alex · Key 10 · the Compass

“And you hide in your room. So, when that neon green rubber band is squeezing your ribs… what would you like to have happen?”

Sam

“I want the rubber band to snap off and turn into a bouncy ball. I want to bounce it.”

Alex · Key 11 · the Blueprint

“You want it to snap off and turn into a bouncy ball. And what needs to happen for that rubber band to turn into a bouncy ball?”

Sam

“I just need to take one massive, deep breath and stretch my arms out as far as they go. It will snap.”

Alex · Key 12 · the System Check

“You need to take one massive breath and stretch your arms out. And can you take a massive breath and stretch your arms out right now?”

Sam

“Yes.”Stands up, takes a long breath, stretches wide. Shoulders drop. “Wow. It just popped. It's a bouncy ball in my hand now.”

Alex

“And it's a bouncy ball in your hand now.”Quietly, letting it settle.

What the phases were doing

Keys 1–5 made the thing visible. Keys 6–9 found where it came from and what it does. Keys 10–12 turned it into a direction, a set of steps, and a test.

Notice what Alex never did: never said “your brother's wrong”, never suggested the bouncy ball, never told Sam to breathe. Every move came from Sam.
