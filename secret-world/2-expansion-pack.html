Module 2 — The Expansion Pack

Level 2: Advanced Mapping, Glitch Traps, and Teleporting

Welcome to Level 2!

If you mastered the basic Secret World Explorer's Guide, you know how to use the Magnifying Glass and the Map Pin to help your friend look at their own map. You already know the Golden Rule: Never mess with your friend's stuff.

But what happens when your friend's map is really confusing? What if they are caught in a massive puzzle, or two parts of their map are fighting each other?

Real Master Explorers know how to track "Invisible Strings," how to handle "Glitch Traps," and how to use real-life room space to change the camera angle. Here is your advanced toolkit.

1. Finding the Invisible

Strings (How the Map Connects)

(Adults call this: Systemic Relationships & Vectors)

In a video game, if you step on a red button, a secret door opens across the room. They are connected by invisible wiring. The Secret World works exactly the same way. One Landmark usually affects another.

If your friend has a "heavy rock" in their stomach and a "dark cloud"

over their head, they aren't just sitting there doing nothing. They are interacting. Your job is to find the invisible strings connecting them.

The "Wiring" Questions

"And is there a connection between [Landmark A] and [Landmark B]?"

(Example: "And is there a connection between that heavy rock and that dark cloud?")

"And when [Landmark A] is doing that, what happens to [Landmark B]?"

(Example: "And when that rock gets heavier, what happens to the cloud?")

By asking this, you help your friend see how the puzzle pieces fit together. They might suddenly realise, "Oh! The cloud is actually raining on the rock and making it heavier!" Once they see the wiring, their brain starts figuring out how to rewire it.

2. The Teleporter Pad (Using the Real Room)

(Adults call this: Clean Space Protocol)

Sometimes, your friend gets stuck looking at a problem from the same chair. In their Secret World, it's like their character is pinned in a corner and the camera is stuck.

You can fix this by using a real-world cheat code: Physical Movement. When you move your physical body to a new spot in the room, your brain actually opens up different files and sees things from a new angle.

How to use the Teleporter If your friend is really stuck, stop asking about the map for a second.

01 Look around the actual room you are in.

02 Say: "And if you could move to any other spot in this room to think about this... where would you go?"

03 Let them physically get up and move. Maybe they sit on the floor, stand by the window, or lie on the rug.

04 Once they are in the new spot, ask: "And from here... what do you know now about [The Problem]?"

It sounds like magic, but 9 times out of 10, just standing in a new physical spot changes the whole map in their head!

3. The Glitch Trap

(Adults call this: Double Binds)

The Glitch Trap is the hardest puzzle in the game. It's when your friend is stuck between two bad options.

"If I try to make the team, I might fail and look dumb."

"If I don't try out, I'll be mad at myself forever."

They are frozen. Most people try to give advice here ("Just try out! Who cares what people think!"). Do not do this!

It will just make them defend the trap.

Instead, a Master Explorer maps both sides of the trap and the space between them.

How to map a Glitch Trap 01 Map the first bad thing: "And what kind of 'look dumb' is that?"

02 Map the second bad thing:

"And where is that 'mad at myself forever'?"

03

The Master Question: "And

what is between [Bad Thing 1] and [Bad Thing 2]?"

By forcing them to look at the space between the two traps, they usually find a hidden path or a safe zone they didn't know was there.

4. The Autosave (Letting the Map Update)

(Adults call this: Landscape Maturing)

This is the most important rule of Level 2.

Let's say you ask a great question, and suddenly your friend's "block of ice" melts into a "warm puddle." Your friend takes a deep breath and smiles.

DO NOT yell "Yay! We fixed it! Let's go get snacks!"

In a video game, when you beat a boss or unlock a new area, the screen says Saving... Do not turn off your console.

If you turn it off too fast, you lose your progress.

When your friend's map makes a big, happy change, their brain is Autosaving. You have to give it time to load the new map.

How to trigger the Autosave When a big change happens, talk slowly and quietly.

"And now it's a warm puddle. And when it's a warm puddle... what happens to the rest of the map?"

"And is there anything else about this warm puddle?"

"And as you look at this warm puddle, what do you know now?"

Let them enjoy the safe zone for a few minutes. Let the new code settle into their brain. Only when they look totally relaxed and say they are done, is the mission actually over.

Let's Play: A Master Level Conversation A full example showing all the advanced tools in action.

The Conversation Begins

Friend "I just feel completely torn in half about this argument with Sam. I want to apologise, but I also want to protect myself because they were mean." (A Glitch Trap!)

Explorer "And you feel torn in half. And when you are torn in half, what kind of torn in half is that?"

Friend "It's like two ropes pulling my chest in opposite directions."

Finding the Invisible Strings Explorer "And two ropes. And is there a connection between the rope that wants to apologise, and the rope that wants to protect you?" (Finding the Invisible Strings)

Friend "Yeah... they are both tied to the same knot right in the middle of my ribs."

Using the Teleporter Pad Explorer (Noticing the friend looks really tense sitting on the bed). "And they are tied to a knot. And if you could move to any spot in this room to look at that knot, where would you go?" (The Teleporter Pad)

Friend "Um... I want to stand up and look out the window."

(Friend walks to the window and looks out).

Explorer "And from there, looking out the window... what do you know now about that knot?"

Friend "From here... I can see that the knot isn't actually that tight. If I just drop my hands, the ropes kind of fall away. I don't have to pull on either of them right now. I can just wait."
