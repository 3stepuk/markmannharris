Module 4 · Solo Mode

The Solo Explorer's Survival Kit

How to hack, map and upgrade your own Secret World.

When the game crashes

You are officially a Master Explorer. You know how to guide your friends through their Secret Worlds without messing up their maps.

But what happens when you are the one with a “heavy rock” in your stomach, or a brain full of “buzzing bees”?

Exploring your own map just by thinking about it is really hard. If you try to fix a glitch by sitting there and thinking, your brain usually gets stuck in an infinite loop.

To play Solo Mode you have to get the map out of your head and into the real world so you can look at it. Here are five cheat codes for doing exactly that. The first one has nothing wrong in it at all, and it is the one to learn first.

Cheat Code 0: The Home Base

For the days when nothing is wrong. Adults call this Modelling a Resource Space.

Most people only open the map when something has gone wrong. That is like only looking at a game when it crashes — you never learn what it looks like when it is working. So cheat code zero is the one you use before any of that: pick a place that holds, and map it.

You need: nothing. You can do this in your head, on a bus.

01 · Pick the place

Somewhere steady — a bench, a shed, the smell of a car, the top of the stairs. Not because it is a nice place. Because you chose it.

02 · Give it a shape

Ask yourself: “And what kind of place is that place?” A light, a colour, a sound, a smell. A place you can see is a place you can find again.

03 · Find it in your body

Ask: “And where do I feel that place?” Chest, hands, feet, shoulders. When you know where it lands, you know when it has loaded.

04 · Find the way in

Ask: “And what happens just before I get there?” A door, a smell, a voice at the gate. That is the handle.

05 · Keep it

Ask: “And what would help me find my way back?” A word, a smell, a picture. Something small enough to carry.

That is the whole thing, and it takes ten minutes. You now have somewhere to stand, whether or not anything is ever wrong. Module 0 walks it through with a friend.

Cheat Code 1: The Doodle Quest

For everyday glitches. Adults call this Clean Self-Modelling via Art.

When your thoughts are a tangled mess, the fastest way to untangle them is to draw them. You don't need to be a good artist — stick figures and messy scribbles actually work best.

You need: a blank piece of paper and some coloured markers or pencils.

01 · Find the glitch

Think about the thing that is bothering you. Ask yourself: “When I am worried, mad or stuck about [X]… what is that like?”

02 · Draw it

Immediately draw the first thing that pops into your head on the left side of the paper. If it feels like a “black storm cloud”, draw it.

03 · Magnifying Glass

Look at your drawing. Ask the drawing: “And what kind of [storm cloud] is that?” Add those details — maybe purple lightning bolts.

04 · Quest Compass

Ask yourself: “And when this [storm cloud] is here… what would I like to have happen?”

05 · Draw the upgrade

Draw your answer on the right side of the paper. Maybe you draw a bright, warm sun.

06 · Find the trail

Look at both drawings. Ask: “And what needs to happen for this [storm cloud] to become that [warm sun]?”

07 · Autosave: draw whatever comes to mind in the middle of the paper connecting them. Watch how your brain fixes the puzzle right on the paper.

Cheat Code 2: The Sticky Note Floor Map

For huge puzzles. Adults call this Clean Space Protocol.

Sometimes a problem feels too big to fit inside your head. You need a bigger screen. You can turn your bedroom floor into a giant, real-life map using sticky notes.

You need: a pack of sticky notes (or scraps of paper) and a pen.

01 · Name the pieces

Think about your messy problem. Write the different parts of it on separate sticky notes — “Maths homework” on one, “Wanting to play Xbox” on another, “Feeling tired” on a third.

02 · Place the landmarks

Stand in the middle of your room. Take your first sticky note and ask: “Where does this belong?” Put it exactly where your gut tells you.

03 · Walk the map

Physically walk around your room and look at your sticky notes.

04 · Change the camera angle

Stand somewhere random — in the closet, or on your bed looking down. Ask: “And from up here… what do I know now about all these sticky notes?”

05 · Move the pieces

Ask: “Does anything need to move?” Let your hands move the notes wherever they want to go. When they all feel right, the game is saved and you will know exactly what to do next.

Cheat Code 3: The Two-Hand Balance

For Glitch Traps. Adults call this Somatic Double-Bind Resolution.

Are you completely torn between two things? “I want to go to the sleepover, but I am scared I will get homesick.” This is a Glitch Trap. Your brain is pulling you in two opposite directions.

You need: just your two hands.

01 · Load hand one

Hold out your left hand. Put the first part of the trap in it. “This hand is wanting to go to the sleepover.” Notice how that hand feels. Heavy? Light? Hot? Cold?

02 · Load hand two

Hold out your right hand. Put the second part in it. “This hand is being scared of getting homesick.” Notice how this hand feels.

03 · Weigh them

Hold both hands out in front of you, like you are holding two invisible boxes. Look back and forth between them.

04 · The Master Question

Stop looking at your hands. Look right at the empty space in the middle. Ask: “And what is between this hand, and that hand?”

05 · Let the engine work

Just stare at the empty space. Don't force anything. Usually your hands will start to move on their own — together, apart, turning over. When they stop moving, the trap is unlocked.

Cheat Code 4: The Portal Generator

For preparing for tomorrow. Adults call this Self-Future Pacing.

You figured out the puzzle. You used the tools, and now your “heavy rock” has turned into a “light, glowing shield.” You feel great. But how do you make sure you remember to take this shield to school tomorrow?

You need: your imagination.

01 · Find the safe zone

Focus on the good, strong or calm feeling you have right now — your “glowing shield”.

02 · Open the Portal

Ask yourself: “And as I take this [glowing shield] with me… what is the very first thing I will notice when I walk into the school building tomorrow?”

03 · Throw it through

Imagine yourself walking into school tomorrow holding that shield. What does it look like? How do you walk?

04 · Log off

Take a deep breath. You just planted a save-point in the future. When you walk into school tomorrow, your brain will automatically load the shield for you.

The Solo Explorer's Promise

Being a Solo Explorer means you are the developer, the player and the guide of your own life. Remember:

My World Engine is smart. Even if I feel totally stuck, my brain knows how to fix it if I give it the right tools.

I don't have to force it. If a puzzle isn't solving itself right away, I can leave my map on the paper and come back to it later.

I am the boss of my own map.

Grab your tools. Your Secret World is waiting.
