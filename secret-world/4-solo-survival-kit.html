Module 4 — The Solo Survival Kit

How to Hack, Map, and Upgrade Your Own Secret World You are officially a Master Explorer. You know how to guide your friends through their Secret Worlds without messing up their maps.

But what happens when your game crashes? What happens when you are the one with a "heavy rock" in your stomach, or a brain full of "buzzing bees"?

Exploring your own map just by thinking about it is really hard. If you try to fix a glitch just by sitting there and thinking, your brain usually gets stuck in an infinite loop.

To play Solo Mode, you have to get the map out of your head and into the real world so you can look at it. Here is your Solo Explorer's Survival Kit. It contains four "Cheat Codes" you can use to map your own mind.

Cheat Code 1: The Doodle Quest (For Everyday

Glitches)

(Adults call this: Clean Self-Modelling via Art)

When your thoughts are a tangled mess, the fastest way to untangle them is to draw them. You don't need to be a good artist—stick figures and messy scribbles actually work best!

Inventory needed: A blank piece of paper and some colored markers or pencils.

How to play: The Doodle Quest 01 Find the Glitch Think about the thing that is bothering you. Ask yourself:

"When I am worried/mad/stuck about [X]... what is that like?"

02 Draw it Immediately draw the first thing that pops into your head on the left side of the paper. If it feels like a "black storm cloud," draw it.

03 Use the Magnifying Glass Look at your drawing. Ask the drawing: "And what kind of [Storm Cloud] is that?" Add those details to your picture. (Maybe you add purple lightning bolts).

04

The Quest Compass

Ask yourself: "And when this [Storm Cloud] is here... what would I like to have happen?"

05 Draw the Upgrade Draw your answer on the right side of the paper. (Maybe you draw a bright, warm sun).

06 Find the Trail Look at both drawings. Ask yourself: "And what needs to happen for this [Storm Cloud] to become that [Warm Sun]?"

07 Autosave Draw whatever comes to mind in the middle of the paper connecting them. Watch how your brain fixes the puzzle right on the paper.

Cheat Code 2: The Sticky Note Floor Map (For

Huge Puzzles)

(Adults call this: Clean Space Protocol)

Sometimes a problem feels too big to fit inside your head. You need a bigger screen. You can turn your bedroom floor into a giant, real-life map using sticky notes.

Inventory needed: A pack of sticky notes (or scraps of paper) and a pen.

How to play: The Sticky Note Floor Map 01 Name the Pieces Think about your messy problem.

Write down the different parts of it on separate sticky notes.

(Example: Write "Math Homework" on one, "Wanting to play Xbox" on another, and "Feeling Tired" on a third).

02 Place the Landmarks Stand in the middle of your room.

Take your first sticky note and ask:

"Where does this belong?" Put it exactly where your gut tells you.

Maybe "Math Homework" goes under your desk. Maybe "Xbox"

goes on the door.

03 Walk the Map Physically walk around your room and look at your sticky notes.

04 Change the Camera Angle Stand in a completely random spot—maybe in the closet, or stand on your bed looking down. Ask yourself: "And from up here... what do I know now about all these sticky notes?"

05 Move the Pieces Ask yourself: "Does anything need to move?" Let your hands move the notes wherever they want to go. When all the notes feel like they are in the "right" spot, the game is saved, and you will know exactly what to do next.

Cheat Code 3: The Two-Hand Balance (For

Glitch Traps)

(Adults call this: Somatic Double-Bind Resolution)

Are you completely torn between two things? "I want to go to the sleepover, but I am scared I will get homesick."

This is a Glitch Trap. Your brain is pulling you in two opposite directions.

Inventory needed: Just your two hands.

How to play: The Two-Hand Balance 01 Load Hand 1 Hold out your left hand. Put the first part of the trap in it. "This hand is wanting to go to the sleepover."

Notice how that hand feels. Is it heavy? Light? Hot? Cold?

02 Load Hand 2 Hold out your right hand. Put the second part of the trap in it. "This hand is being scared of getting homesick." Notice how this hand feels.

03 Weigh Them Hold both hands out in front of you, like you are holding two invisible boxes. Look back and forth between them.

04

The Master Question

Stop looking at your hands. Look right at the empty space in the middle of your hands. Ask yourself: "And what is between this hand, and that hand?"

05 Let the Engine Work Just stare at the empty space. Don't force anything.

Usually, your hands will naturally start to move. They might slowly come together, one might drop, or they might turn over. Let your body do whatever it wants.

When your hands stop moving, the trap is unlocked.

Cheat Code 4: The Portal

Generator (For Preparing for Tomorrow)

(Adults call this: Self-Future Pacing)

You figured out the puzzle. You used the tools, and now your "heavy rock" has turned into a "light, glowing shield." You feel great! But how do you make sure you remember to take this shield to school tomorrow?

Inventory needed: Your imagination.

How to play: The Portal Generator 01 Find the Safe Zone Focus on the good, strong, or calm feeling you have right now. (Your "glowing shield").

02 Open the Portal Ask yourself: "And as I take this [glowing shield] with me... what is the very first thing I will notice when I walk into the school building tomorrow?"

03 Throw it through Imagine yourself walking into the school tomorrow holding that shield. What does it look like? How do you walk?

04 Log off Take a deep breath. You just planted a save-point in the future. When you walk into school tomorrow, your brain will automatically load the shield for you.

The Solo Explorer's Promise

Being a Solo Explorer means you are the developer, the player, and the guide of your own life. Remember:

My World Engine is smart.

Even if I feel totally stuck, my brain knows how to fix it if I give it the right tools.

I don't have to force it. If a puzzle isn't solving itself right away, I can just leave my map on the paper and come back to it later.

I am the boss of my own map.

Grab your tools. Your Secret World is waiting!
