Module 5 — The Logbook

Every Master Explorer needs to study the logs of those who came before them. When you are out in the field, things happen fast. Sometimes your friend's map changes in an instant. Sometimes a Glitch Trap is really stubborn.

Here are five real missions, straight from the Explorer's Logbook. They show exactly how kids just like you used their tools to level up, starting from the basics and going all the way to Master Level Solo Mode.

Mission 1: The Math Monster

Explorer Level: Level 1 (Basic Tools)

The Glitch: Mia is staring at her math homework and starts crying. She says, "It's just too much! It feels like a giant weight."

The Exploration

Explorer "And it's too much, and it feels like a giant weight. And where is that giant weight?" (The Map Pin)

Mia "It's pushing right down on my shoulders."

Explorer "Pushing down on your shoulders. And what kind of giant weight is that giant weight?"

(The Magnifying Glass)

Mia "It's a huge, grey concrete block. It's so heavy I can't even lift my pencil."

Explorer "And it's a huge, grey concrete block, and you can't lift your pencil. And when that concrete block is on your shoulders... what would you like to have happen?" (The Quest Compass)

Mia "I just want it to break into pieces so I can move."

Explorer "And you want it to break into pieces. And is there anything else about breaking into pieces?" (The Flashlight)

Mia (Takes a deep breath and sits up slightly).

"Yeah. If I just do the first math problem, the block cracks a little bit. If I do another one, it crumbles into sand."

The Upgrade: The Explorer didn't say, "Math isn't that hard," or "Just do the first problem." The Explorer just helped Mia map the "concrete block." Once Mia's World Engine realized the block could crumble into sand, she picked up her pencil and did the homework.

Mission 2: The Hallway

Freeze Explorer Level: Level 1 & 2 (The Rewind Button & Invisible Strings)

The Glitch: Leo and his friend Jayden had a misunderstanding. Now, every time they see each other in the hallway, Jayden acts weird.

Jayden says, "I just freeze up. It's so awkward."

The Exploration

Explorer "And it's awkward, and you freeze up. And what happens just before you freeze up?" (The Rewind Button)

Jayden "I see Leo walking toward me, and this spiky invisible wall pops up between us."

Explorer "And a spiky invisible wall pops up. And is there a connection between Leo and that spiky wall?" (Finding the Invisible Strings)

Jayden "No... the wall is actually coming from me. It's like a porcupine. It pops up because I'm scared he's still mad at me."

Explorer "And it pops up because you're scared he's mad. And when that spiky wall is there like a porcupine... what would that spiky wall like to have happen?" (Using the Quest Compass on the Landmark)

Jayden "It just wants to protect me. But it doesn't need to be spiky. It could just be a soft bubble."

Explorer "And it could just be a soft bubble. And when it's a soft bubble... what happens to freezing up?" (The Autosave)

Jayden (Smiles). "I don't freeze. I can just wave at him."

The Upgrade: By hitting the Rewind Button, the Explorer helped Jayden find the trigger (the spiky wall). By finding the connection, Jayden realized the wall was trying to help, but needed a "soft bubble" upgrade.

Mission 3: The Blank Screen

(Using the Real Room)

Explorer Level: Level 2 (The Teleporter Pad)

The Glitch: Sam is trying to write an English story, but is totally stuck.

"My brain is empty. It's just a blank screen."

The Exploration

Explorer "And your brain is empty, and it's a blank screen. And where is that blank screen?"

Sam "It's literally right in front of my nose. I can't see anything else." (The camera angle is too close!)

Explorer "Right in front of your nose. And if you could move to any spot in this bedroom to look at that blank screen, where would you go?" (The Teleporter Pad)

Sam "I'd go sit on the windowsill, far away from my desk." (Sam gets up and sits on the windowsill).

Explorer "And from there, sitting on the windowsill...

what do you know now about that blank screen?"

Sam (Looking at the desk from across the room).

"Oh. It's not just a screen. It's a movie screen.

And the projector is actually behind my chair.

The screen is blank because I haven't turned the projector on yet."

Explorer "And you haven't turned the projector on yet.

And what needs to happen to turn the projector on?"

Sam "I just need to write down one terrible idea to get the motor running."

The Upgrade: Sam was stuck because the "screen" was too close to his face in his Secret World.

Just by physically walking to the window, his brain's camera angle zoomed out, allowing him to see the whole system (the projector) and figure out how to turn it on.

Mission 4: The Soccer Tryout Trap

Explorer Level: Level 2 (The Glitch Trap)

The Glitch: Chloe is panicking about the soccer team. "If I try out, I might not make it and I'll be embarrassed. But if I don't try out, I'll be furious with myself. I'm completely torn in half."

The Exploration

Explorer "And you're completely torn in half. Let's map it. Put 'trying out and being embarrassed' in your left hand. What does that hand feel like?"

Chloe "It's heavy. Like a freezing cold rock."

Explorer "And a freezing cold rock. Now put 'not trying out and being furious' in your right hand.

What does that feel like?"

Chloe "It's hot. Like a buzzing hornet."

Explorer "A cold rock here, and a buzzing hornet there.

Hold them both out." (Chloe holds out her hands). "Now, look at the empty space right in the middle. And what is between the cold rock and the buzzing hornet?" (The Master Question)

Chloe (Stares at the empty space for a long time).

"There's... a path. A grassy path."

Explorer "And a grassy path. And what kind of grassy path is that?"

Chloe "It's just a path where I go out and kick the ball because I love playing. It has nothing to do with the team or the coach." (Chloe's hands slowly drop to her sides). "I don't have to carry the rock or the hornet. I can just walk on the grass."

The Upgrade: The Glitch Trap was paralyzing Chloe. If the Explorer gave advice ("Just do your best!"), Chloe would have kept holding the rock and the hornet. By looking at the space between the traps, her World Engine rendered a brand-new safe zone (the grassy path).

Mission 5: The Solo Stage

Fright (Master Boss Battle)

Explorer Level: Level 3 (Solo Mode & The Portal)

The Glitch: Maya is alone in her room. Tomorrow she has to sing a

solo at the school concert. She feels sick to her stomach with anxiety.

Her World Engine is crashing, and she has to be her own guide.

The Exploration (Solo Mode)

01

The Doodle Quest

Maya grabs a piece of paper. She asks herself, "When I am terrified about the solo, what is that like?" She draws a tight, jagged knot of black scribbles in the center of the page.

02

The Magnifying Glass

She asks the drawing, "What kind of black knot is that?" She realises it's a knot of electric wire. She draws yellow lightning bolts coming off it.

03

The Quest Compass

She asks, "When this electric knot is here, what would I like to happen?" On the other side of the paper, she draws a bright, glowing, smooth yellow lightbulb.

04

The Autosave

She asks, "What needs to happen for the knot of wire to become the lightbulb?" She stares at the paper. Suddenly, she realises the wire doesn't need to disappear—it just needs to plug into the bulb!

She draws a plug connecting them. She takes a massive deep breath. Her stomach instantly settles.

The electricity is now powering the lightbulb.

Opening The Portal Maya feels great, but the concert is tomorrow. She needs to save her game.

Maya (Closes her eyes). "And as I take this glowing lightbulb with me... what is the very first thing I will notice when I walk onto the stage tomorrow?"

(The Portal Generator)

Maya (Answers out loud). "I will notice the microphone stand shining in the spotlight, and I will feel the electricity run down my arms and into my feet."

The Upgrade: Master Level achieved! Maya used art to untangle her own Glitch Trap. Then, by asking the Portal question, her brain time-travelled to the concert and planted the lightbulb on the stage. The next night, when she walked out, the lightbulb was waiting for her.
