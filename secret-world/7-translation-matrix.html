Module 7

The Translation Matrix

Bridging clinical Clean Language and pre-teen teaching — the Rosetta Stone.

This is the key for practitioners, educators and parents. It maps the technical terminology of Clean Language, Symbolic Modelling and Systemic NLP directly onto the Secret World metaphor.

By comparing them side by side, a facilitator can be sure the rigour of the original method stays intact while being said in a language a ten-year-old can use.

1. Foundational epistemology

Clinical termThe Secret WorldWhat it does
Autogenic Narrative / Metaphor
(self-generated internal landscape)		The Secret MapEveryone has a unique, custom-built level in their head. The player is the sole developer of that space.
Perceptual Filtering / Constructivism
(the map is not the territory)		Three Worlds, One DogTwo people can hold the same thing in completely different ways, and neither is wrong. The discovery is that other people’s maps are not copies of your own.
Non-Contamination
(excluding the facilitator's assumptions)		The “No Littering” RuleDon't drop your own code, advice or ideas into someone else's game. Unwanted advice is messing up their save file.
Epistemological Distance
(perceptual distance from the phenomenon)		The Camera AngleIf a problem is too scary, they don't have to fight it; they zoom the camera out and look at it safely from above.
Psychoactive Landscape
(a space that responds to attention)		The World Engine / Live MapThe inner world isn't a static drawing; it's a live engine. Looking at a glitch with the right tool can make it re-render.

2. Structure, vectors and space

Clinical termThe Secret WorldWhat it does
Node
(an atomic metaphorical anchor)		The LandmarkConverts abstract feelings into concrete objects — a heavy rock, a dark cloud — that can be located and changed.
Vector
(a direction or line of implication)		The Trail / Invisible StringsTracks where a feeling came from, where it's going, or how one landmark is secretly wired to another.
Problem Loop (The Egg)
(a closed, self-referencing threat circuit)		The GlitchNormalises being stuck. The child isn't broken; their game has hit a repeating loop that needs debugging.
Resource Space (The Chicken)
(latent generative areas)		The Safe ZoneA place on the map that already holds. It is noticed, not installed. Module 0 models it before there is anything to fix.
Somatic Endpoint
(physical manifestation of a structure)		Body CluesWatch for real-world updates — a deep sigh, relaxing shoulders — to know the inner engine has processed new code.
Binding / Double Binding
(two opposing forces locking the system)		The Glitch TrapBeing paralysed by two bad choices. Don't force a choice; look at the space between the traps to unlock it.

A note for practitioners

While the language of the Secret World is gamified, the mechanics underneath are strictly systemic. A practitioner can review a session conducted with a pre-teen and translate the child's “Autosave” or “Glitch Trap” straight back into formal notes — Landscape Maturing and Double-Bind Resolution — so that while the teaching is playful, the container stays phenomenologically sound.
